## Final Thought

Your article is strong because it's **honest**. Pick a title that reflects that honesty:
- Own the speedup (Option 1)
- Own the insight about "right tool" (Option 4)
- Own the problem you're solving (Option 2)

Avoid:
- ❌ Hype ("Revolutionary!", "Game-changing")
- ❌ Vague ("Building an AI Agent")
- ❌ Clickbait (sensational but not true)

You've got the goods. Pick a title that shows it.
